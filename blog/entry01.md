## Why Space?

The topic I chose for my year-long Freedom Project is **Space and the Universe**. I chose this topic because I am interested in space and how big our universe really is. Space is something that we have all thought about at some point in our lives, and we are curious about it. There are so many things to learn about in space. It contains planets, stars, galaxies, and black holes. I also want to learn about new things that I haven't explored before. I want to learn more about the eight planets in our solar system. How old are they? How big are they? What makes each planet different? I think it is interesting that there is so much beyond Earth that we still don't know about.

## EDP

Right now, I am on Step 1 (Defining) and Step 2 (Researching) of the EDP (Engineering Design Process). First, I had to decide what I was interested in and what I wanted to learn about for my year-long project. I chose Space and the Universe because I have always been curious about space and how big our universe really is. Now, I am doing more research and learning about all the different things in space. There is so much to learn, and I find it really interesting. I think I will be on Step 2 for a while because I want to learn as much as I can before moving on to the next step.
## Research Sources

Some of the main sources I explored were [NASA's Solar System Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/solar-system-facts/), which had all the facts about our solar system, and it was very interesting.

Next, [Wikipedia's Outer Space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_space). This included all the information about the planets around us, and it gave us a deep understanding of the planets, different vocabulary words, and a detailed scientific version of information about outer space.

[National Geographic's Space Resource Library](https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/resource-library-space/) had everything about different planets and different things in space.

## Skills

One skill I'm developing is researching for this project. I have to search for information about space and find different websites that provide useful facts. I have to find websites that have valid facts and websites that I know have the right information and are trustworthy sources. For example, NASA. We all know that NASA is a trustworthy website because they have rockets that go to space and spaceships. They have everything. NASA. Wikipedia is another one we could trust because everybody uses Wikipedia for their facts, and it is a well-known website. So, it is really helping me learn which websites are right and about the different websites. And the more you research, the more you learn.

Another skill I'm developing is reading and understanding information. Some articles contain certain words and information that I really was not familiar with, especially the vocabulary words. The space topic has a lot of vocabulary words that I found quite difficult, but it was kind of interesting. I read carefully and understand what they are explaining instead of just reading the information without understanding it. This also helped me explain what I learned in my own words and created a new curiosity for space.
