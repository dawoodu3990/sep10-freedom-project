## Part A: Existing Technology

##### Software


- 1st Google search: "Astronomy software and space programs"
- 1st website looked into: [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/)
  - This website has information about space, planets, stars, and galaxies.
  - It also has different tools that people can use to explore space.
  - First software I looked at was [NASA Eyes](https://science.nasa.gov/eyes/)
    - [NASA Eyes picture and examples](https://science.nasa.gov/eyes/)
    - This software lets people explore space in 3D.
    - You can look at planets, moons, and asteroids.
    - It shows different NASA spacecraft and space missions.
    - You can see where spacecraft are going and learn about their missions.
    - It helps people understand our solar system better.

  - Second software I looked at was [Celestia](https://celestiaproject.space/)
    - [Celestia pictures](https://celestiaproject.space/)
    - This software lets people explore the universe in 3D.
    - You can look at planets, stars, moons, and galaxies.
    - You can zoom in on different objects in space.
    - It lets you travel from one planet to another.
    - It helps people learn about different objects in the universe.

  - Third software I looked at was [AutoStakkert!](https://www.autostakkert.com/)
    - [AutoStakkert sample pictures](https://www.autostakkert.com/wp/samples/)
    - This software helps people make clearer pictures of space.
    - It takes many pictures of the same object and combines them.
    - It looks for the clearest parts of each picture.
    - It can be used for pictures of planets, the Sun, and the Moon.
    - It helps people see more details in their space pictures.
    - It is mainly used by people who take pictures of space using telescopes.

  - "Programs used by astronomers"
