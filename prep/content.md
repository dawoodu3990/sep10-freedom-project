The topic I chose for my year-long Freedom Project is Space and the Universe. I chose this topic because I am interested in space and how big our universe really is. There are so many things to learn about, including planets, stars, galaxies, and black holes.

## Part A: Existing Technology

##### Software

- First software I looked at was [NASA Eyes](https://science.nasa.gov/eyes/)
  - Lets people explore planets, moons, and other objects in our solar system in 3D.
  - Shows NASA spacecraft and different space missions.
  - [NASA Eyes picture](https://science.nasa.gov/eyes/)

- Second software I looked at was [Celestia](https://celestiaproject.space/)
  - Lets people explore the universe in 3D.
  - Shows planets, moons, stars, and other objects in space.
  - [Celestia picture](https://celestiaproject.space/)

- Third software I looked at was [AutoStakkert!](https://www.autostakkert.com/)
  - Combines multiple pictures of planets, the Moon, and the Sun.
  - Selects the clearest parts of images to create a better picture.
  - [AutoStakkert picture](https://www.autostakkert.com/)
